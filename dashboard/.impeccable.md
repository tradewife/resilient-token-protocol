## Design Context

### Users
Hackathon judges and Solana developers evaluating an autonomous treasury protocol. They are technical, time-constrained (3 minutes), and skeptical. They need to see working infrastructure — not marketing fluff — presented as something they haven't seen before.

### Brand Personality
Sovereign, organic, vigilant. The protocol is alive — it runs forever, funds itself, and evolves. It is not a dashboard for humans to "manage" anything. It is a window into an autonomous organism.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Tone**: Botanical research station meets institutional surveillance — a living system observed through precision instruments. The background image (ethereal motion-blurred flower in deep emerald and coral) establishes the primary palette: deep forest greens grounded against warm coral accents.

**Theme**: Dark. This is an autonomous trading system observed by engineers. Deep emerald void, not cold tech-black.

**References**: Warp terminal's restraint and warmth, but translated through a green-coral complementary palette. The flower image provides the emotional core — organic intelligence, not mechanical.

**Anti-references**: Crypto casino dashboards, purple-blue neon, glassmorphism vaults, "Web3" gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. **Show the organism, not the controls** — viewers observe an autonomous system; they are not "managing" it
2. **Restraint through nature** — the flower image provides all decorative interest; the UI itself is precise and quiet
3. **Data has weight** — numbers earn their size; small labels earn their opacity
4. **Green is home** — every neutral tints toward emerald; coral appears only at peak moments
5. **Earned complexity** — what's visible at first glance is simple; depth reveals on interaction
