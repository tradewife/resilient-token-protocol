## For Token Creators

If your launchpad supports RTP, enabling the treasury is one click. No code, no wallet setup, no SDK installation required.

### How It Works For You

1. **Launch your token** on a supported platform (Pump.fun, Bags.fm, or Raydium)
2. **Enable RTP** — your launchpad adds an "Enable RTP Treasury" button during or after launch
3. **That's it** — trading fees begin flowing to your token's treasury vault PDA
4. **Swarm activates** — the autonomous swarm monitors the treasury and executes strategies when capital is sufficient
5. **Yield flows back** — generated yield is redistributed: 70% to holders, 20% to you (project dev), 10% to ecosystem

### What You Get

- **Program-enforced treasury** — no one can sign funds away from the PDA vault (not even the team)
- **Autonomous yield** — strategies validated on 30,000 configs/night, executed on Hyperliquid (USDC-margined)
- **On-chain redistribution** — the 70/20/10 split happens automatically when yield exceeds the runway threshold
- **Phase evolution** — as the treasury grows, it evolves: Sustenance → Ecosystem → Humanity Fund

### Supported Platforms

| Platform | Best For | Fee Routing | Guide |
|----------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Pump.fun | Memecoins, community tokens | Creator fees → keeper claims to PDA | [Pump.fun](/docs/integration/pump-fun) |
| Bags.fm | Creator-focused tokens, fee sharing | Multi-claimer fee split to PDA | [Bags.fm](/docs/integration/bags-fm) |
| Raydium | DeFi tokens, AMM LP bootstrap | Creator redirect to PDA | [Raydium](/docs/integration/raydium) |

> **No RTP Token Required** — RTP is infrastructure, not a token. You keep your own token. RTP wraps it with treasury functionality.
